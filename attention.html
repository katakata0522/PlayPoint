To help you get the most accurate estimate for your own Google Play account, there is one quick heads-up I'd love to share with you.

Play Points level thresholds, base earn rates, currency units, and available top levels can differ by country. PlayPoint currently has dedicated calculator modes for Japan, the United States, South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and India—each tuned to its own regional calculation profile.

Important: Translation does not change calculation rules. If you use browser auto-translate on the Japanese page, it still calculates using Japanese yen rules instead of your own region's rates (and translating the Taiwan page into English still leaves Taiwan's Play Points rules in place). Open the regional mode that matches the Play country on your account.

Promotion rates

When Google Play shows a special earn rate, enter the final regional earn rate shown in the offer details. The calculator compares that rate with the normal rate for your current level and uses the higher one. It does not multiply the level rate by the promotional number.
