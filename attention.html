Country & Region Guide

Choose the calculator by your Google Play account country, not only by browser language. Play Points level thresholds, base earn rates, currency units, and available top levels can differ by country.

PlayPoint currently has dedicated calculator modes for Japan, the United States, South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and India. Each mode uses its own regional calculation profile.

Important: Translation does not change calculation rules. For example, translating the Taiwan page into English still leaves Taiwan's Play Points rules in place. Open the regional mode that matches the Play country on your account.

Promotion rates

When Google Play shows a special earn rate, enter the final regional earn rate shown in the offer details. The calculator compares that rate with the normal rate for your current level and uses the higher one. It does not multiply the level rate by the promotional number.

Official verification links

Google can change Play Points conditions. Before making a purchase, check the current conditions shown in Google Play and the official regional Play Points help page.
