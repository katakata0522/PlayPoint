After you reach a new level, Google says you keep it through the end of the next calendar year. At the start of each year, the level can change based on the points earned during the previous calendar year. The safest way to plan is to separate your spendable balance from your annual level progress. Reaching it in January and reaching it in December therefore produce different lengths of time, but both end at the close of the next calendar year. This is why the date shown in your own Google Play level-benefits screen matters more than a generic countdown. Country availability, level thresholds, benefits, and earning rates can differ, while the account screen shows the rule applied to you. Balance and annual level progress are two different numbers Your Play Points balance is the amount available to exchange for eligible rewards. Level progress is the total of qualifying points earned during the current calendar year. One can go down while the other stays unchanged. A high balance does not prove that you earned enough this year to keep the same level next January. Likewise, a low balance does not necessarily mean your annual progress is low if you already spent points on rewards. Using points does not erase level progress Google states that using or exchanging points deducts them from your Play Points balance, but it does not deduct them from level progress. You do not need to preserve the balance only to protect the points you already earned toward a level. Important exception: if you return or cancel a purchase that earned points, Google may deduct those points from both the balance and level progress. Check the points history after a refund instead of relying on an earlier screenshot. What happens at the January review At the start of a calendar year, Google may change your level according to the number of points earned in the previous calendar year. If that annual total meets the requirement for your current level in your country, you can begin the new year at that level. If it does not, the account can move to the level supported by that total. Do not subtract the minimum threshold of an inherited level by yourself. For planning, use the remaining points displayed in Google Play. Local thresholds and earning rates are not universal, so another country's amount should not be copied. Where to check before the end of the year Open the Google Play Store, tap the profile icon, open Play Points, then open More and Level benefits. Google says this screen can show the current level, benefits, base earning rate, points needed for the next level, and how long the current level remains. Also open Points history when a recent purchase, promotion, cancellation, or refund could change the annual total. Record the date and the displayed remaining points before using a calculator. Weekly prizes, support, and other benefits do not have guaranteed cash value, so the level label alone should not decide the purchase.
