Google Play payment failed in India? Check the UPI or card order

Published and checked against official sources on November 28, 2026.

When a UPI app shows a debit but your game still shows the purchase button, start with the order. In the Play Store, open your profile, then Payments & subscriptions and Budget & history. Use the Google Account that received the purchase email. The order status tells you whether to investigate payment or item delivery.

A UPI purchase or a subscription renewal?

Google Play India supports UPI across providers. For a one-off item, match the amount and time in your UPI app with the Play order. For a subscription, review the UPI AutoPay mandate as well. The mandate sets the amount, frequency or duration of recurring payments. A successful one-off UPI payment on the same account does not tell you whether a renewal mandate is active.

If the payment method itself is missing

Google lists RuPay cards for one-time purchases. If a renewal is failing, look at the options offered for that subscription, including UPI AutoPay where supported. Google discontinued Netbanking as a Play payment method in October 2025 until further notice. For a declined card, check its expiry date, billing details and available funds with your bank.

If your bank shows a debit and the item is missing

If the Play order is pending, keep the receipt and UPI transaction reference while you check its status. If the order is complete, close and reopen the game on the account that should receive the item. The developer can trace delivery using the Play order number. For a payment-profile alert, use the Google payments center.

Can I look up a game publisher’s web-store order in Google Play?

Check the payment receipt for the seller. A purchase billed on the publisher’s own website is handled by that store. Play order history covers purchases billed by Google Play.
