India Google Play Points Guide: Levels, ₹5 Earn Rates & Perks

Verified against Google Play India help documentation on 2026-11-19

India's Google Play Points rules differ sharply from the US or Japan profiles: base points are earned per ₹5 INR spent before taxes, and Google's official India table lists four levels (Bronze through Platinum) rather than a Diamond tier. Official India Play Points Levels & ₹5 Earn Rates

Points earned in a calendar year (January 1 to December 31) determine your status. Once you reach a higher level, you keep it until the end of the next calendar year.

How ₹5 INR Calculation & Rounding Work

Google multiplies the pre-tax item price by your level's base earn rate and rounds up or down to the nearest whole number. Very small purchases below a minimum amount can earn 0 points.

  • Bronze example: Spending ₹25 INR on an eligible Google Play purchase earns 5 points (₹25 ÷ ₹5 × 1 = 5).
  • ₹5 is the earn unit, not cash value: Earning 1 point per ₹5 does not mean 1 point equals ₹5 in Play credit. Always check the Use tab in Google Play to see how many points an in-game coupon or Play credit reward requires.
  • Redeeming points never lowers your level progress: Using points deducts from your redeemable balance only. Refunds or canceled subscriptions do deduct the earned points from both balance and level progress.

Eligible Purchases, Expiry, Country Switch & Offers

  • Eligible platforms & billing: Works on Google Play purchases across Android, computer (Google Play Games on PC), and smart TVs when billed through Google Play's billing system (apps, games, in-app purchases, subscriptions, Google One from Android, and books).
  • PC-only vs shared promotions: Offers labeled "on PC" apply strictly to computer purchases, not phone purchases. Multiple promotions never stack on a single transaction; Google automatically applies your highest active earn rate.
  • 12-month inactivity expiration & country change: Points expire 12 months after the last date you earned or redeemed points. Changing your Google Play billing country forfeits existing points, and your tier does not transfer.
  • Platinum Premium Support: Platinum members receive priority queue handling for Play Store phone or live chat inquiries and access to dedicated specialists, subject to support availability.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many points do you earn per rupee in India Google Play Points?

Google Play Points in India calculates base points per ₹5 INR spent excluding taxes: Bronze earns 1 point per ₹5, Silver earns 1.1 points per ₹5, Gold earns 1.2 points per ₹5, and Platinum earns 1.4 points per ₹5, rounded up or down to the nearest whole number.

Is Diamond tier available in Google Play Points India?

Google's official India Play Points table currently lists four levels: Bronze (0–249), Silver (250–999), Gold (1,000–3,999), and Platinum (4,000 or above). Platinum is the highest listed tier in India and includes Premium Support.

When do Google Play Points expire in India?

Points expire 12 months after your last activity where you earned or used points. If you change your Google Play billing country, unused points are lost and your tier does not carry over.
