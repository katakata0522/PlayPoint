Google Play Points weekly rewards: what to claim on Thursday and Friday

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-16 Date details

Start with the name of the benefit

If you've ever wondered why Google Play gives you a reward on Thursday one week and asks you to come back Friday the next, here's the quick split: the regular Play Points weekly prize unlocks at Silver and resets every Friday, while Play Pass weekly bonuses and boosters refresh on Thursdays for eligible subscribers and family members. Thursday and Friday refer to different benefits, so claiming one doesn't mean the other vanished.

If you're Gold, Platinum, or Diamond and see a Super Weekly card instead, follow the eligibility, stock notes, and claim window shown right on that Perks card.

Regular weekly prize: Silver and above

To find your regular weekly draw, open Google Play → Profile icon → Play Points → Perks. Under current US rules, this benefit starts once you hit Silver (150 annual points) and resets every Friday in your Play country. Bronze members don't have a weekly prize yet. (Google’s official guidance)

Exact point amounts vary by tier and week, and Google doesn't publish fixed payout odds—so take another player's lucky screenshot as an example of what's possible rather than a guaranteed weekly return.

Play Pass Thursday benefit is separate

If you subscribe to Google Play Pass (or share an eligible family plan), you get a separate weekly Play Points bonus or booster that refreshes every Thursday. That Thursday Play Pass drop sits on the Play Pass side and doesn't replace—or count as—your Friday Play Points prize.

Watch the deadline on the Play Pass card: an unclaimed Thursday perk usually gets overwritten when the next week's benefit rolls in.

How to read Super Weekly Prize cards

Google's US level table lists Super Weekly Prize for Gold (600 points), Platinum (3,000 points), and Diamond (10,000 points). When it's live for your tier, the card in your Perks tab spells out any extra rules, remaining stock limits, and how long you have to claim.

Keep in mind that prize lineups and rollouts vary by Play country—so a Super Weekly screenshot from Korea, Taiwan, or Japan won't necessarily match what appears on a US account.

How to troubleshoot a missing weekly reward

First match the day and screen to the right perk: check your Play Points tier and Perks tab for Friday's regular or Super Weekly prize, and check the Play Pass offers area for Thursday's subscriber bonus.

If you tapped a card and the animation glitched out, check Play Points → Points history instead of searching for the card again—Points history records whether the points actually landed.

Claiming a Play Pass bonus on Thursday and then seeing a different card on Friday is totally normal; they're two separate perks, so read the card title before assuming you got a duplicate. On the flip side, having claimed Thursday's Play Pass bonus won't explain a missing Friday Play Points prize.

When an expected card genuinely never shows up, verify which Google account you're signed into (multiple Gmail accounts on one Android phone are the most common culprit) and check your claim history. If everything looks eligible and nothing arrived, take a screenshot of your tier status and contact Google Play support—don't make extra purchases just to test whether the card triggers.

Tip if you track your rewards: log the actual points shown in Points history rather than guessing from a promo banner. If you win a coupon or physical prize instead of points, jot down the prize name separately so your point-earning diary stays accurate from week to week.

FAQ

Can Bronze claim the regular weekly prize?

No—under current US Play Points rules, the regular Friday weekly prize starts once you reach Silver (150 annual points). While you're at Bronze, you can still earn points through purchases, featured app installs, and Play Quests, or check whether Play Pass offers any subscriber perks if you're a member.

Why does one reward refresh Thursday and another Friday?

They belong to two different programs. Thursday is the weekly refresh for Play Pass subscribers (and eligible family members) who have an active Play Pass points bonus or booster, while Friday is the regular Play Points weekly prize unlocked at Silver and above. If you qualify for both, you can claim both each week.

Is Super Weekly guaranteed for every US account?

No—while Google's US level table lists Super Weekly Prize starting at Gold, individual promo drops depend on limited prize stock and any account-level eligibility terms on the card. Checking the Perks tab after Friday midnight local time is the only way to see what's live for your account that week.
