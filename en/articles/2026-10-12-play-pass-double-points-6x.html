Play Pass double points and 6x boosts: US Play Fest 2026 recap

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-28 Date details

Published October 12, 2026 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Official info checked October 16, 2026

Saved articles & reading history

The main Play Fest event ended on October 19, 2026. This is a record of the US Play Pass double-points and 6x booster offers advertised from October 8 through the festival. Current benefits have their own cards and terms; this record does not promise that the festival offer is still available.

On October 16, the US event page listed 2x weekly Play Pass bonuses: 40 points plus a 6x boost, alongside a daily 3 PM Happy Hour with a 12-hour boost and book offers for October 16–18. Those offers do not combine into one multiplier. Your own card shows its eligibility and remaining time.

Two benefits, not a 12x purchase rate

Google’s announcement uses both “double points” and “6x boosts.” Do not multiply the labels together. Google’s earning rules apply the highest eligible earn rate instead of stacking different special earn rates.

Play Pass has a separate weekly points-bonus program in the US as well as purchase boosters. A points grant adds the amount on its card to your balance; a booster changes the earn rate for eligible purchases. The event page ties the 2x label to the weekly bonus; its advertised 40 points do not establish eligibility for every account. Use the final rate on your purchase card when estimating earned points.

Thursday bonuses are separate from Friday rewards

Google’s Play Pass weekly bonuses refresh on Thursday and unclaimed bonuses expire the following Thursday. The amount can vary from week to week. They are separate from the regular Play Points weekly prize, which refreshes on Friday in your Play country.

A member’s level alone does not establish a guaranteed weekly amount. This US guide therefore does not turn an individual account’s points result into a promise for all subscribers.

Check the booster before the purchase

Open Google Play with the account that will make the purchase, then look in Play Pass for the current benefits. If the booster card requires activation, activate it before paying. Check eligible purchases, the final earn rate and the card’s expiry.

The campaign announcement covered Play Fest, whose advertised dates were October 13–19. Even during the festival, that did not mean an activated booster lasted for every remaining day: its own card set the usable window. Afterwards, Points history is the place to check what was credited.

Paid subscriptions, trials and family accounts

The published weekly bonus terms and booster terms require an individual subscriber with an active Payments account and an auto-renewing paid Play Pass subscription. They exclude family members, prepaid plans, trial subscriptions and accounts on payment hold, and specify randomized eligibility during testing. They also describe a mobile-only scope within Play Pass games and apps.

There is a discrepancy worth checking before subscribing: Google’s weekly-rewards Help page includes family-group members, while those linked terms exclude them. We cannot promise that a family member or trial user qualifies for the festival’s double-points or 6x card. Read the terms attached to your own offer, including eligible titles and purchases; do not assume every purchase qualifies.

Game discounts have their own rules: $0 trials and promotional periods do not qualify until paid billing starts, and family game offers are for the manager. Keep these separate from the points card rather than using one rule for every benefit.

Use the offer where it fits

If you already subscribe and have a purchase in mind, checking the booster first can make that purchase more rewarding. The US Play Fest recap records the festival offers and explains the separate November 2 sweepstakes deadline. A new subscription does not bring back the festival promotion.
