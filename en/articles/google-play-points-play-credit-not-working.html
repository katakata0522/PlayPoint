Can’t redeem or use Google Play credit? Start with these checks

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-14 Date details

Published July 25, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · Official info checked July 25, 2026 · Play Points troubleshooting

Saved articles & reading history
First, check whether the issue happens before or after you redeem your points. If the Play credit option won't let you tap it in the Use tab, it usually comes down to point balance or regional availability. If you already swapped points for credit and can't spend it at checkout, look at expiration, Google account mismatch, country/currency lock, or what you're trying to buy.

Before redeeming: points and reward availability

Open Play Points and tap the Use tab on the account you want to spend from. If the Play credit card is grayed out, you may not have enough points for that reward yet.

Play credit denominations and availability also vary by Play country and account, so an exchange option you saw in an online screenshot from another region might not appear in yours.

After redeeming: expiration and balance check

Once you trade points for Play credit, that promotional credit expires after one year. Keep that separate from the 1-year inactivity timer on your unredeemed Play Points balance itself.

If your Points history shows the redemption went through but your balance looks empty at checkout, make sure the Play Store and the in-app payment sheet are both using the exact same Gmail account that redeemed the credit.

Account and currency mismatches at checkout

Google Play balance cannot be moved or shared between Gmail accounts. On an Android phone with multiple profiles, Android sometimes opens the checkout sheet under your primary device account instead of the one holding your Play credit.

Your Play balance also has to be spent in the same currency as the content you're buying. If your Play country, payment profile, or item currency doesn't line up, Play balance won't show up as a payment option.

If you recently changed your Play country

If you changed your Play country, any remaining Play balance from your previous country cannot be used in the new country—it stays locked to your old country profile.

Unfortunately, you can't transfer that old balance to a different account or cash it out. Review your old payment profile and any existing subscriptions tied to that country before loading new funds.

Eligible items and splitting payment at checkout

Google Play balance covers eligible apps, games, books, in-app items, and subscriptions on Google Play, but it can't buy physical hardware from the Google Store or top up Google Pay or another payment method.

If your item costs more than your remaining Play balance, many supported purchases let you combine your Play balance with another payment method on the checkout screen—just make sure the Play balance toggle is switched on before paying. Also remember that exchanging points for Play credit is final and non-refundable.

Sort out the block before spending again

Pinpoint why the credit didn't apply before topping up or making a backup purchase, then estimate only the spending you actually planned.

FAQ

Why is the Play credit reward grayed out?

When the Play credit option looks grayed out, your active Google account usually doesn't have enough spendable points for that reward tier yet. Double-check that you're viewing the right Gmail account in the Play Store, and note that available reward denominations depend on your Play country and account.

When does exchanged Play credit expire?

Google Play credit redeemed from Play Points expires after one year from the date you make the exchange. Keep that separate from your unredeemed Play Points balance, and since point-to-credit redemptions aren't refundable, it's best to convert points only when you have a purchase in mind.

Can I use old balance after changing country?

No—once you switch your Play country, any Play balance left in your old country stays tied to that previous country and currency and can't be spent in the new one. You also can't transfer it to another account or cash it out, so check your old payment profile if you're tracking down where it went.
