Game Web Stores & External Billing: Do They Earn Play Points?

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Published 2026-11-19 Date details

Published November 19, 2026 · Updated November 19, 2026 · Official info checked November 19, 2026 · Play Points guide

Saved articles & reading history
Many mobile games promote official web stores with bonus gems or extra bundles. Before assuming those purchases still help your Google Play Points progress, check which billing system actually processes the payment.

Table of contents

  2. Alternative in-app billing excludes Play Points and Play balance

  4. Refund support also follows the processor

Official publisher store does not mean Google Play billing

A game developer’s official web shop is an official sales channel for that game, but browser checkouts paid directly to the publisher or an external processor bypass Google Play’s billing system. Do not expect Play Points from those browser transactions.

Alternative in-app billing excludes Play Points and Play balance

Google’s billing policy states that purchases processed through an alternative billing system cannot earn Google Play Points, cannot use Google Play balance or gift cards, and are not eligible for Play offers.

How to compare an external bonus against Play Points fairly

Compare the same dollar budget side by side: count guaranteed extra in-game items or discounts on the publisher web store on one side, and Google Play’s regular/boosted points plus Play coupons on the other—without treating Play Points and in-game currency as equal 1:1 numbers.

Refund support also follows the processor

Transactions completed outside Google Play’s billing system are managed by the developer or alternative payment provider, not Google Play. Check the seller’s support and refund terms before paying on an external site.

FAQ

If a web shop is run by the official game studio, will I still get Play Points?

No, unless checkout runs through Google Play’s billing system. Official publisher web stores paying the studio or a third-party gateway directly do not count toward Play Points.

Can I spend Google Play balance on an official game web store?

No. Alternative billing and external browser checkouts do not accept Google Play balance or Google Play gift cards.
