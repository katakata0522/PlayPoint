P&D Pass: daily dungeons, the free trial and when it is worth keeping

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

What to choose

Consider Puzzle & Dragons’ P&D Pass if you will clear its scheduled dungeons and use the monthly benefits. If you only need Magic Stones for a banner today, compare a direct pack first. Eligible US accounts can use the one-week trial to see whether the pass fits their routine before keeping the monthly renewal.

Start with the product

P&D Pass

A monthly, automatically renewing subscription with scheduled exclusive dungeons and other benefits. It is a different purchase from individual Magic Stone packs.

Why a month of benefits is different from a Stone pack

Look at the current pass dungeon schedule and the rewards you plan to clear. A reward that arrives after your target banner ends should not be included in that banner budget. Other pass benefits can still matter, but give them value only if you will use them.

The US publisher FAQ lists US$8.99 per month, not a converted Japanese ¥980 figure. It is a published reference price; use Shop → P&D PASS in your Android client for the amount you will actually be charged.

The trial is a chance to test your routine

The US free trial lasts one week and is available once per Puzzle & Dragons account. It renews into a paid subscription unless canceled. Use the trial to see whether you actually clear the pass content, rather than keeping it just because the first week was free.

Cancel through the app store that billed the pass when you do not want the next renewal. Your pass expiry and the dungeon schedule are different dates; note both when planning rewards.

Put your own budget into the comparison

Start with the amount you already planned to spend. For each product, list what arrives now and what you can collect before your deadline. That shows which rewards actually help your next purchase or banner.

Use the current Android checkout price for this comparison. A listing from another store can help identify the product, but its price may belong to a different platform or region.

Where Play Points fit

Eligible Google Play purchases earn Play Points. Spending game currency you already own does not create another purchase, and an official web checkout is billed separately. Use the final earn rate shown on your Google account; future points are a separate benefit from the game resources delivered today.

FAQ

Where do I find the current Android P&D Pass price?

Open Shop → P&D PASS in the US Android client. The publisher FAQ lists US$8.99 as its published reference, while your checkout shows the actual charge.
