US Google Play Points levels: requirements, rates and benefits

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-14 Date details

Published July 7, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · Official info checked August 5, 2026 · United States guide

On a US Google Play account, Silver starts at 150 annual points, and every step up raises your base earning rate and unlocks new perks. The table below covers all five US tiers from Bronze to Diamond—use it alongside the annual progress counter inside your Play Points screen.

Official US level table

LevelAnnual pointsRegular earning rate
Bronze0–1491 point per $1

Gold600–2,9991.2 points per $1
Platinum3,000–9,9991.4 points per $1
Diamond10,000 or more1.6 points per $1

The numbers above track your annual level points, not your spendable balance. Redeeming points for Play credit or coupons lowers your available balance, while your annual level progress stays intact. Refunds or canceled orders are the exception—those can deduct points from both.

Why tier thresholds aren't fixed dollar totals

Because each tier unlocks a higher earn rate along the way, a threshold like 600 or 3,000 points doesn't translate to a single flat spending figure. You earn 1 point per $1 at Bronze, and as soon as you cross into Silver or Gold, subsequent purchases earn at the higher rate—plus special earn-rate promos can get you there with much less spending.

Google Play also calculates points on each pre-tax item separately and rounds to the nearest whole point. If you want exact numbers on a big order, check the point preview on the checkout sheet and your Points history afterward.

Weekly prizes and how tier timing works

Silver members unlock the regular Friday weekly prize in their Play country, while Gold and above gain access to the Super Weekly Prize. If you subscribe to Play Pass, you also get a separate Thursday bonus or earn-rate booster.

Whenever you reach a new tier, you keep it through the end of the next calendar year, after which your status updates each January based on the points you earned during the calendar year that just closed.

How to plug these numbers into the calculator

Start with the remaining annual points shown on your Play Points level screen—never your spendable point balance. Select your current US base earn rate first, then switch to a confirmed special earn rate only when you're pricing out a purchase that actually qualifies for that promotion.

Keep in mind that these numbers apply specifically to the United States Play country; markets like Korea and Taiwan use completely different currencies, earn units, and tier thresholds.

FAQ

How many points are needed for Silver in the US?

You need 150 annual level points earned within a calendar year to unlock Silver on a US Google Play account. At Bronze's base rate of 1 point per $1 before sales tax, that works out to about $150 in qualifying purchases, though featured promos and quests can trim that down.

Does spending points lower my level progress?

No—cashing in points for Play credit, coupons, or game items only reduces your spendable balance and leaves your annual tier progress intact. The only thing that takes points away from your level progress is a refund or canceled purchase.

Is Diamond 10,000 points in every country?

No. The 10,000-point Diamond threshold and 1.6 points per $1 base rate shown on this page apply specifically to the United States Play country. Regions such as Korea and Taiwan use different currencies, earn units, and point thresholds, so always follow the numbers displayed in your own Play Store app.
