US level guide

US Google Play Points levels: requirements, rates and benefits

United States guide ・ 2026-08-05

This English guide uses the United States Play country, not Japanese, Korean or Taiwan thresholds. Silver starts at 150 annual points in the US, while the regular earning rate begins at 1 point per $1 before tax. Use the level progress shown in your own Play Points screen as the final number.

Official US level table

Diamond10,000 or more1.6 points per $1

The points shown in this table are annual level points. Your redeemable balance is a different number. Redeeming points does not reduce the annual level progress, while refunds or cancellations can remove points connected to the purchase.

Base spending estimates from zero

At the Bronze base rate, Silver is about $150, Gold about $600, Platinum about $3,000 and Diamond about $10,000 in eligible item price before tax. These are planning estimates, not fixed invoices, because promotions and rounding can change the result.

Each item is calculated separately and rounded to the nearest whole point. The purchase preview and Points history should decide the final result.

Weekly benefits and level timing

The regular weekly prize begins at Silver and resets on Friday in the Play country. Play Pass weekly bonuses and boosters are a separate Thursday benefit for eligible subscribers and family members.

Level progress is based on points earned during the level period shown by Google Play. Check the app near year-end rather than assuming a lifetime total controls the next year.

How to use the calculator

Enter the points still missing, not your current redeemable balance. Use the US earning rate displayed for your level, then compare a confirmed offer only when the purchase is eligible.

The US figures on this page must not be copied to another Play country. Korea and Taiwan use different thresholds, currencies and earning units.

FAQ

How many points are needed for Silver in the US?

150 annual level points.

Does spending points lower my level progress?

No. Redeeming points lowers the usable balance, not the annual level progress. Refunds and cancellations are different.

Is Diamond 10,000 points in every country?

No. This page is for the United States Play country only.
