Do subscriptions earn Google Play Points?

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-14 Date details

Published July 7, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · Official info checked August 3, 2026 · Play Points guide

Saved articles & reading history
Yes—eligible subscriptions billed through Google Play can earn Play Points once your account is enrolled in the program. What earns points (and at what rate) depends on your Play country, tier, and whether a promo applies to that billing cycle. When planning toward a tier goal, count only completed eligible charges rather than free trials or an entire year of renewals you haven't paid for yet.
Scope of this guide: Here we focus on recurring subscriptions, renewals, free trials, and Google Play billing. If you're wondering about buying gift cards or spending Google Play balance instead, see whether gift cards earn Play Points.

Which subscriptions actually earn Play Points

Google lists eligible subscriptions right alongside apps, games, and in-app purchases as ways to earn Play Points. The key rule is the billing path: the subscription has to be billed directly through Google Play on the account that joined Play Points and qualify under your Play country's rules.

If you subscribe directly on a publisher's website using a credit card or third-party checkout instead of Google Play billing, don't include it in your points math—external billing doesn't earn Play Points.

Separate the first charge from renewals

The price you pay for a subscription isn't always the same from month to month. You might start with a 7-day free trial, pay a discounted intro rate for month one, and then roll onto the standard monthly renewal price. Treat each billed cycle on its own rather than copying month one's points forever.

If you're budgeting for your next bill, plug in that upcoming pre-tax renewal price. Only count a full 12 months of points if you genuinely plan to keep the subscription active all year.

Trials, intro discounts, cancellations, and refunds

Free trials with a $0 charge won't earn points—you only start earning when a real, eligible payment settles. Intro discounts, grace periods, partial prorated adjustments, and refunds all change the net points attached to that cycle.

Once a charge goes through, you can verify the exact points awarded under Play Points → Points history. Keep in mind that simply turning off auto-renew doesn't take past points away, whereas getting an actual refund for a billed charge deducts those points from both your balance and annual level progress.

Why promos may not cover every renewal

Time-limited point boosts often apply only to new subscriptions, your very first paid cycle, or charges that hit during a specific promo window—meaning next month's renewal drops back to your normal US tier rate (1 to 1.6 points per $1).

Run your baseline plan at your regular tier rate first, and only apply a special promo earn rate to a charge you know is covered by an active offer on your account.

Use subscriptions you already keep as a pace check

Existing monthly subscriptions (like a game pass or app membership you already rely on) are a handy way to estimate steady progress toward US Silver (150 points) or Gold (600 points) without spending anything extra.

Just don't subscribe to new services purely to chase a tier—if your normal spending leaves a big gap, paying for extra subscriptions almost always costs far more than the tier perks are worth.

FAQ

Can subscriptions earn Google Play Points?

Yes. Eligible subscriptions billed through Google Play earn Play Points once your Google account is enrolled in the program. Just double-check that you're paying through Google Play billing rather than an external web checkout, since off-Play subscriptions don't earn points.

Do all subscription renewals earn the same amount?

Not always. An introductory discount, a price change, a one-time promo boost on the first billing cycle, or a tier upgrade later in the year can all change how many points a renewal earns. Check your pre-tax renewal price and current tier rate for each cycle.

Can a free trial earn points?

No—a $0 free trial doesn't generate any Play Points on its own. Points start accruing only when the trial converts into an eligible paid charge and that transaction settles. You can confirm the exact credit in Points history once you're billed.
