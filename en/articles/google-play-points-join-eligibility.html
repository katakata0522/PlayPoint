Can't join Google Play Points in the US? Eligibility checks

Published July 25, 2026 · Updated September 11, 2026 · Official info checked September 11, 2026 · United States guide · 2026-09-11

Short answer

Google Play Points is available in the United States. If the Join button is missing, first check the account against Google's signup requirements rather than creating another payment profile or account.

The quickest order is: Play country → account management and age → payment method and billing address → correct signed-in account → Google support.

Six checks before you troubleshoot

  • The United States is listed among Google's supported countries.
  • You are using a compatible Android device or computer.
  • The Google Play account is not managed by a school or a parent.
  • You meet Google's age requirement; the current learning guide says Play Points is for users age 18 or older.
  • The account has a valid payment method.
  • The billing address matches the Play country.

Check the signup requirements first

Google's public signup guidance combines country, device, account management, age, payment method and billing information. Passing only one of those checks is not enough.

CheckWhat should match
Play countryUnited States on the Google Play account you intend to use.
AccountNot managed by a school or parent; workplace accounts depend on their actual management policy.
Age18 or older under Google's current Play Points learning guidance.
PaymentA valid payment method with a billing address that matches the Play country.
DeviceA supported Android device or computer.

On Android, make sure you are signed into the account that will actually make purchases, then open Profile → Play Points. Membership and balance belong to the account, not to the device as a whole.

If you recently changed Play country

Google says a Play country update can take up to 48 hours. Check the active Play country and payment profile first; do not create a second payment profile only to make the Join button appear.

If you use several Google accounts on one device, confirm which account owns the payment profile and which account is open in Google Play.

Contact Google when the requirements match

If the requirements match, the correct account is selected, and the relevant country-change window has passed, use Google's Play Points signup troubleshooting from the affected account.

Before contacting support, note the account email, current Play country, payment profile country, device type and a screenshot of the missing menu. Keep order numbers and payment details out of public posts.

Still no Join button?

Continue with Google's sign-up troubleshooting from the same account. That is the next task for this page; calculating points comes later, after membership is available.

FAQ

Is Play Points available in the United States?

Yes, but the account and payment profile still need to meet the joining requirements.

Are all work accounts blocked?

Google's public requirements do not state that. Managed accounts may have restrictions, so check the account administrator and Play services settings.

Is a missing Join button the same as missing purchase points?

No. Join eligibility and transaction tracking are separate issues.
