Can't join Google Play Points in the US? Eligibility checks

United States guide ・ 2026-08-05

Google Play Points is available in the United States, but the Join button can still be missing when the Play country, payment profile, account management or device context does not meet the program requirements. Work through the checks in this order instead of treating every missing menu as the same problem.

The supported countries list includes the United States. The account must not be managed by a school or a parent, and its billing address must match the Play country.

Official requirements to join

Use a supported Play country, a compatible Android device or computer, a Google Play account that is not managed by a school or parent, a valid payment method and a billing address matching the Play country.

A workplace account is not automatically disqualified by the public rule. The important question is whether the account is managed in a way that restricts Play services.

Check the Play country and payment profile

Open Google Play settings and confirm that the Play country is United States. The billing address and payment method must align with that country.

If you recently changed country, Google says the update can take up to 48 hours. Do not create a second payment profile only to make the button appear without first checking the active profile.

Confirm the account and menu

Switch to the Google account that will actually make purchases, then open Profile > Play Points. Family devices often contain several accounts, and each account has its own Play Points membership and balance.

Update the Play Store and try the web Play Points page when the app menu is stale. A missing Join button is different from points missing after a completed purchase.

When to contact Google

Collect the account email, current Play country, payment profile country, device type and a screenshot of the missing menu. Do not post order numbers or payment details publicly.

If the program still does not appear after the requirements match and 48 hours have passed, contact Google Play support from the affected account.

FAQ

Is Play Points available in the United States?

Yes, but the account and payment profile still need to meet the joining requirements.

Are all work accounts blocked?

Google's public requirements do not state that. Managed accounts may have restrictions, so check the account administrator and Play services settings.

Is a missing Join button the same as missing purchase points?

No. Join eligibility and transaction tracking are separate issues.
