Can't join Google Play Points in the US? Eligibility checks

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-14 Date details

Published July 25, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · Official info checked September 11, 2026 · United States guide

Saved articles & reading history

Short answer

Yes—Google Play Points is live across the United States. When the Join button isn’t showing up on your device, check the account against Google's signup requirements before creating an extra Google Account or second payment profile.

Checking in this order solves most cases fastest: Play country → account type & age → payment method & billing address → active signed-in account → Google support.

Check the six signup requirements first

Even though the United States is one of Google’s supported countries, Play Points won’t let you enroll unless your account satisfies all six eligibility checks together. Your account cannot be managed by a school or a parent, you must meet Google’s 18+ age rule, you need a supported Android device or computer, and your payment method must have a valid US billing address.

On Android, double-check which Google Account is selected in the top-right profile menu before opening Play Points. Enrollment belongs to each individual Google Account—not your phone as a whole—so switching accounts changes whether the Join button appears.

If you recently changed your Play country

Google notes that a Play country switch can take up to 48 hours to finish updating across your account. Let that window pass and double-check your active Play country and payments profile first rather than spinning up a second profile just to chase the Join button.

If you keep multiple Google Accounts on one phone, verify which account actually owns the US payment profile and make sure that same account is active in the Play Store.

When everything matches, contact Google support

Once your US Play country, age, personal account status, and billing address all check out—and any 48-hour country update has finished—run through Google’s Play Points sign-up troubleshooting while signed into the affected account.

Before starting a chat, jot down the account email, current Play country, payment profile country, device model, and a screenshot showing the missing menu (while keeping private payment numbers out of public posts).

Still no Join button?

Walk through Google’s official sign-up troubleshooter on the affected account. Once you’re enrolled, come back to estimate points and tier progress in the calculator.

FAQ

Is Play Points available in the United States?

Yes—the United States is an active Google Play Points country, so the program itself is available nationwide. When the Join button doesn’t show up on a US phone, it usually means one of the account-level checks (Play country, account management, 18+ age, or US billing address) still needs attention.

Are all work accounts blocked?

Google’s public requirements exclude accounts managed by a school or a parent, without stating a blanket ban on every work account. However, company-managed Google Workspace accounts can carry admin restrictions—check with your IT administrator if Play Points isn’t enabled on a work profile.

Is a missing Join button the same as missing purchase points?

Those are two separate situations. Missing the Join button means the account hasn’t enrolled in Play Points yet, whereas missing points on a purchase you already made after joining comes down to account mismatch, payment method, or processing time—our missing-points guide covers that check.
