How to earn Google Play Points without a new purchase

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Answer: check account benefits before adding a purchase

Yes, you can pick up Google Play Points without spending money—just not every day, and it depends on your tier and active offers. Start with your weekly prize if you are Silver or higher, then check the Earn tab for featured app installs or no-spend quests. If you have a brand-new Bronze account, it is completely normal to see zero free offers on a given day.

Other point-earners—like purchase quests, special earn-rate promos, and Play Pass bonuses—are worth grabbing too, but they require spending money or keeping a paid subscription. Everything available to your US account lives inside the Earn, Perks, and Play Pass screens in the Google Play Store.

Three ways to earn points without buying anything

1. Your weekly reward

On US accounts, reaching Silver unlocks weekly prizes inside the Perks tab. They refresh every Friday in your Play country, so it takes five seconds to claim before the weekend. If you can't remember whether you already tapped this week's button, look at your Points history.

2. Super Weekly Prize

Once you hit Gold or higher, Perks can also feature a Super Weekly Prize card with higher-tier rewards or Super Tickets. Tap the current card to see what's in the pool before claiming. Keep in mind that spending a Super Ticket to reroll replaces your first prize rather than adding to it—our Super Weekly Prize guide breaks down how that trade-off works.

3. Featured app or game installs

Every so often, Google Play displays a featured install card that awards points for trying out a specific free app or game. It only applies to the exact title shown on that card—not just any free download from the store—so watch the deadline, first-install rule, required playtime, and personalization settings closely.

Under Google’s US install-offer conditions, a standard first-install promo requires at least two minutes of use within 24 hours of installation, and the points can take up to seven days to show up. Keep those two clocks separate: opening the app late misses the offer, while waiting a few days for points to post is normal.

If you installed the app on time and the points still haven't landed after seven days, our install reward troubleshooting guide covers first-install eligibility and what screenshots to grab before reaching out to support.

Watch out for quests that require spending

Quests mix all kinds of tasks—installing a game, signing in, playing for a few minutes, subscribing, or spending a set dollar amount. Seeing a quest card doesn't automatically mean it's free.

  1. Read every step and the end date before tapping to start.
  2. Scan for minimum spend amounts, purchase counts, subscriptions, or title-specific rules.
  3. If a step requires buying something, only move forward if it lines up with a purchase you already planned.
  4. Once finished, verify progress on both the quest card and your Points history.

One easy trap to avoid: purchases paid with Play Points don't count toward purchase quests, and getting a refund on a qualifying order can pull back your quest progress or reward.

Play Pass Thursday perks (for existing subscribers)

Eligible US Play Pass subscribers and family members can receive a Thursday Play Points bonus or an earn-rate booster, separate from the regular Friday weekly prize.

If you already pay for Play Pass because you play the included games and apps, checking in on Thursdays is an easy habit to build. If you're thinking about subscribing just to farm points, though, weigh the full subscription cost against the library value—treat any Thursday Play Points as a nice bonus rather than a guaranteed monthly rebate.

Already planning a purchase? Stack your checks first

There is nothing wrong with earning points on something you were going to buy anyway. Before hitting pay, glance at the Earn tab to make sure you didn't miss an app-specific boost you need to activate or an active quest for that same title, then double-check your Points history afterward.

Red flags for fake “free Play Points” offers

  • It asks for your Google password or two-factor verification code.
  • It promises unlimited points, instant cash payouts, or point transfers between accounts.
  • It tells you to sideload an unofficial APK, browser extension, or remote-access tool.
  • It asks for a payment or gift card PIN to "unlock" your points.

Real Play Points offers live directly inside the Google Play app or Google's official promotions. No third-party generator or website can inject points into your account.

A simple weekly check-in routine

  1. Thursday: grab your Play Pass bonus if you subscribe.
  2. Friday: open Perks and claim your weekly prize (plus Super Weekly Prize if you are Gold or above).
  3. Right before spending: check Earn for featured installs, quests, and activatable boosts.
  4. Afterward: glance at Points history to make sure everything posted to the Google account you expected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I earn Play Points without spending money?

Yes, though options vary by tier and account—your best shots are Friday weekly prizes (starting at Silver), featured free-app install offers, and no-purchase quests in the Earn tab. Keep in mind that Google Opinion Rewards works differently: on Android, surveys pay out in Google Play credit rather than Play Points. If you later spend that Play credit on an eligible app or in-game item, that separate purchase can earn Play Points under standard rules.
