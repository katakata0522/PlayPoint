Keeping your level

Google Play Points level reset: how long your level lasts

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-16 Date details

Your level doesn't simply vanish at midnight on December 31. When you unlock a new Play Points tier, Google Play lets you keep it through the end of the next calendar year. Every January, your status is reviewed based only on the points you earned during the calendar year that just ended—which is why keeping your spendable balance separate from your annual tier progress makes planning much easier.

The short answer: a new tier lasts through the next calendar year

Whenever you climb to a higher tier during a calendar year, you hold that status until the end of the following calendar year. Earning Gold in February gives you nearly two full years at Gold, while hitting it in November still carries you all the way through December 31 of the next year.

Because regional thresholds and dates depend on your Play country, the expiration date printed right inside your Play Store level screen is always the one to trust.

Redeemable balance and annual level progress are separate

It helps to treat your Play Points account as having two independent counters. Your Play Points balance is the spendable stash you cash in for Play credit, coupons, or in-game packs, while your annual level progress tracks the total qualifying points you've earned since January 1 of the current year.

Because those two numbers move separately, sitting on a big point balance carried over from last year doesn't mean you're safe for next January. On the flip side, spending your balance down to zero doesn't wipe out the tier progress you've built up this year.

Spending your points won't drop your tier

Exchanging points for rewards only subtracts from your redeemable balance—it never touches your annual level progress. There's no reason to hoard points out of fear that cashing in a coupon will bump you down a tier.

The one catch: if you return or cancel a purchase that originally awarded points, Google Play may pull those points back from both your balance and your level progress. Whenever an order gets refunded, give your Points history a quick look so your numbers aren't based on an older screenshot.

What happens during the January review

At the start of each new year, Google Play looks at how many qualifying points your account earned during the calendar year that just finished. If your annual total meets your current tier's threshold in your Play country (for example, 3,000 points for US Platinum or 10,000 for US Diamond), you keep that tier for another year. If it fell short, your account adjusts to whichever level that previous year's total qualifies for.

For example, reaching Gold during 2026 keeps that level active through December 31, 2027, and the January 2028 review then checks only the points you earned in 2027. Even if you redeemed every last spendable point along the way, your 2027 earned total still counts in full.

Don't try to guess your remaining gap by subtracting thresholds in your head, and never borrow numbers from another country's rules. Instead, read the exact "points needed" count shown in your Play Store app.

Where to check your exact numbers before year-end

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon, go to Play Points, and tap More → Level benefits. That screen displays your active tier, base earn rate, remaining points needed to retain or reach the next tier, and the exact date your current level runs through.

If you made a recent purchase, joined a special promotion, or had a refund process recently, glance at Points history too before plugging numbers into a spending estimate.

Deciding if chasing maintenance spend is worth it

Before stretching your December budget, compare the remaining point gap against app or game purchases you already planned to make. Run the gap through the calculator at your account's normal earn rate first, and only factor in a special promotional rate if the exact purchase qualifies and you've activated the offer.

If holding onto a badge means spending way past your normal budget, letting the tier step down in January is usually the smarter call. Weekly prizes and support perks don't have a fixed cash payout, so spending $100 just to keep a tier rarely pays for itself.

Check the gap without forcing a purchase

Plug in the remaining points shown on your Play Store level screen, start with your normal earn rate, and treat the dollar figure as a reality check—not a spending goal.

FAQ

Do Google Play Points levels reset every December 31?

Not as an automatic drop every December 31. Any new tier you reach stays active through the end of the next calendar year, and your annual review at the start of a year is based on the points you earned in the previous year. For example, earning Gold in 2026 locks it in through December 31, 2027, before your 2027 total is checked in January 2028.

Will redeeming points make my level go down?

No—spending your points on Play credit, game coupons, or items only reduces your redeemable balance, leaving your annual level progress untouched. The one exception is a refund or canceled purchase, which can reverse earned points from both your balance and your annual level progress.

Which number should I enter in the calculator?

Enter the remaining points needed shown on your Google Play Level benefits screen (Play Points → More → Level benefits). Avoid typing in your spendable point balance or using another country's tier threshold, since regional thresholds and earn rates vary.
