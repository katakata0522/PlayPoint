Play Pass basics

Google Play Pass explained: games, weekly perks and cancellation

Language: English · US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Published 2026-10-15 Date details

Published October 15, 2026 · Updated October 15, 2026 · Official info checked October 15, 2026 · Play Points guide

Saved articles & reading history

Play Pass is easiest to judge when you start with a game you want to try. The subscription opens a catalog of games and apps; weekly offers and Play Points benefits add a different kind of value. Knowing which is which makes the subscription much easier to compare with your usual purchases.

What Google Play Pass includes

Google Play Pass is a subscription to a selected catalog of games and apps, with ads and in-app purchases removed in those catalog titles. A Play Pass badge on an app’s details page identifies the catalog. It does not make every game on Google Play free.

US subscribers can also have eligible offers and Play Points benefits. An offer for a game outside the catalog does not turn that game into an all-inclusive Play Pass title.

Is Play Pass worth it for the games you actually play?

Open Play Pass in the Play Store and find a few titles you would enjoy this month. Compare your subscription price with what you would otherwise buy. The available catalog can change, so a game’s current badge is more useful than an old list of included titles.

If your favorite game appears only under Offers, read the item and expiry first. For example, a discount on one planned purchase can have value; buying an extra item just to use it adds spending. This is a way to compare your options, rather than a claim about a particular US offer.

For family use, separate catalog sharing from perks: the family manager receives Play Pass Offers. Family members enrolled in Play Points may qualify for weekly point bonuses and boosters under their own eligibility rules.

Check Thursday rewards and US Gold eligibility separately

Google lists the United States among the regions for Play Pass weekly Play Points bonuses and boosters. These refresh on Thursdays and have their own claim and expiry rules. A booster affects eligible purchases; it is different from a bonus you claim.

The US Gold-level benefit also has specific requirements: Play Points membership and an active, paid, auto-renewing Play Pass subscription. Google notes US rollout conditions. A $0 trial or prepaid subscription is not eligible for that Gold benefit, and getting it does not add points to your existing balance. Only the subscriber who purchased Play Pass qualifies for this particular perk.

The US Play Fest bonus and boost guide covers the separate limited campaign. This page explains the ongoing subscription, so a campaign multiplier should not be treated as a permanent feature.

Before starting a trial, check the next charge

In the Play Store, open your profile → Play Pass → Get started to view the offer available to your account. Read the displayed trial length, price after the trial and renewal date before joining. Use your US account’s current price; another country’s price or a previous promotion is not your checkout price.

A $0 trial does not include Play Pass Offers until it becomes paid. Gold eligibility is a separate check too. If you want only the trial, cancel before it ends to avoid the next charge. You can still explore the catalog while comparing whether it is worth keeping.

Your billing country and Play country need to match. A work or school account cannot subscribe. Google’s official subscription guide explains the device and account requirements in full.

How to cancel Google Play Pass—and what happens next

  1. Open the Play Store and tap your profile.
  2. Choose Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Play Pass.
  3. Choose Cancel subscription and follow the confirmation steps. Check the displayed end date.

Access continues until the subscription period ends. Installed apps and their data remain on your device, but paid apps can ask you to buy or resubscribe; ads and paid in-app items can return. Saved offers stop being usable when the subscription expires. Gold benefits can end too unless your earned points qualify you for the level.

Uninstalling a game does not cancel Play Pass. Google currently does not offer a pause option. In a shared family subscription, the manager’s cancellation ends shared access at the end of the billing period.

Cancellation and access: Google’s Play Pass subscription guide and Google Play cancellation help.

Pick a game first, then let the perks add value

A catalog title you want to play gives the subscription a clear purpose. If the weekly perks also fit purchases you already planned, they are a useful extra. That is a more satisfying starting point than subscribing to chase an offer with no game in mind.

To compare the rewards afterward, see using Play Points for coupons, credit and rewards.
