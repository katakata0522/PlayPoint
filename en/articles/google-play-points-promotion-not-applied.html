Confirm whether the offer had to be activated

Some promotions are automatic, while others require an activation button before purchase. A purchase completed before activation may not receive the bonus.

Check the campaign screen and Points history on the same account that made the purchase.

Check dates, time zones, and purchase completion

Campaign windows can depend on local dates, store time zones, and when a transaction completes. Pending, cancelled, refunded, or adjusted orders can change the result.

Compare the receipt timestamp with the exact campaign period rather than relying on memory.

Review app, item, and payment eligibility

A campaign may cover only selected apps, games, subscriptions, in-app items, or payment flows. It may exclude trials, promotional credit, refunded purchases, or some gift-card-funded transactions.

If normal points appear but the bonus does not, eligibility is one of the first conditions to check.

Look for caps and partial bonuses

Some offers have a maximum number of bonus points or apply to only part of a purchase. A smaller-than-expected bonus does not always mean the campaign failed.

Confirm the normal points first, then calculate only the extra campaign portion.

Compare normal and promotional estimates

Run the same purchase at the normal rate and at the verified campaign rate. The difference shows which part is actually missing.

The calculator cannot prove eligibility, but it can help you prepare a clear support message with the purchase date, account, campaign, expected bonus, and actual result.

FAQ

Does a campaign banner mean every purchase qualifies?

No. Read the full offer conditions.

Can normal points appear while bonus points are missing?

Yes. The two parts can have different conditions or timing.

Should I make another purchase to test it?

No. Check the existing purchase and campaign first.
