Google Play Points vs eFootball Points: two completely different currencies

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-14 Date details

Published September 13, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · Official info checked October 7, 2026

Saved articles & reading history

What to choose

If you want to buy eFootball Coins, compare the coin pack and its Google Play earn rate. If you want to redeem eFootball Points, use KONAMI’s Point Program instead. Google Play Points cannot be converted directly into eFootball Points, and spending Coins you already own is not a new cash purchase.

Keep three balances on three separate lines

Coins are the game currency, eFootball Points are KONAMI program rewards, and Google Play Points come from eligible Google Play activity. Check the balance needed for your item before deciding to buy a Coin pack.

KONAMI’s claimed eFootball Points expire at the end of the month six months after claim. Spending them before expiry is a different task from earning Google Play Points. The former Match Pass was replaced by Campaign Hub in v5.0, so old Match Pass purchase advice is not current.

Put your own budget into the comparison

Start with the amount you already planned to spend. For each product, list what arrives now and what you can collect before your deadline. That shows which rewards actually help your next purchase or banner.

Use the current Android checkout price for this comparison. A listing from another store can help identify the product, but its price may belong to a different platform or region.

Where Play Points fit

Eligible Google Play purchases earn Play Points. Spending game currency you already own does not create another purchase, and an official web checkout is billed separately. Use the final earn rate shown on your Google account; future points are a separate benefit from the game resources delivered today.

FAQ

Can I convert Google Play Points directly to eFootball Points?

No. They are separate reward systems issued by different companies.
