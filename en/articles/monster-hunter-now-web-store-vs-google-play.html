Should US Monster Hunter Now players buy gems on the web or in-game?

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Published 2026-11-28 Date details

Published November 28, 2026 · Updated November 28, 2026 · Official info checked November 28, 2026 · Play Points guide

If both shops have the item you want, compare the gems you can actually claim on this purchase. The first-purchase tag matters. For a Hunter Pass, the web store bonus works differently from the bonus on an ordinary gem pack.

Compare the two dollar prices on the same pack

Open the official Web Store and the in-game shop on the same game account. Write down the US dollar price and the total number of gems each shop shows, including the first-purchase bonus only if it is available to you. Divide each total by its price. The English help page illustrates regular packs with Japanese prices, so use the numbers on your own two checkout screens for a US comparison.

Is this your first pack or a repeat purchase?

The publisher labels the quantities in its comparison table as first-purchase amounts. The listed packs give the same gem quantities on subsequent purchases in the two stores. For a repeat purchase, compare the current price, any offer on that item and the receipt you would prefer. A larger first-purchase total is no reason to expect a permanent discount.

Hunter Passes have a different bonus

For the Prime Hunter Pass, the publisher lists 500 gems at purchase plus 150 extra in the Web Store, against 500 in the game. The Extreme Hunter Pass has 600 at purchase plus 300 extra on the web. These are the gems delivered at purchase. Compare any daily claims separately, particularly if you won’t log in every day.

When Play Points change your choice

Eligible purchases billed by Google Play can earn Play Points. Purchases through the publisher’s own checkout do not. If Play shows a coupon you can use on this pack, compare the final dollar amount after applying it. If both totals are otherwise equal, the option that gives you rewards you will actually use is the better buy.
