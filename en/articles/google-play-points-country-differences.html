Google Play Points Available Countries: Full List and 6-Region Comparison

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Changing your phone's language doesn't switch your Play Points rules. What decides everything is the Google Play country tied to the account making the purchase—that single setting controls your currency, earn rate, tier thresholds, reward catalog, and promo offers.

Which countries have Google Play Points?

As of November 26, 2026, Google's official enrollment help lists 36 supported countries and regions. This list describes where the program is available, not where our calculator has a regional preset.

  • Americas: Brazil, Chile, Mexico, United States.
  • Europe: Austria, Belgium, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom.
  • Asia-Pacific: Australia, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, New Zealand, Taiwan, Thailand.
  • Middle East and Africa: Israel, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Türkiye (Turkey), United Arab Emirates.

The Philippines is not included in that official list at the time of this check. Eligibility also depends on the Google Play account and its country settings. Check the current list in Google's official Play Points enrollment help before making any account changes.

PlayPointSim currently provides regional calculators for Japan, the US, Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong and India only. Availability elsewhere does not imply identical earning rates, levels or offers.

What translates across countries—and what doesn't

The basic math is the same everywhere: start with how many annual points you're still short, divide by your current earn rate, and factor in any multiplier promo you've activated. What doesn't carry over is the currency, spending unit, or tier threshold—for instance, US Diamond starts at 10,000 points, while Japan, Korea, Taiwan, and Hong Kong set Diamond at 15,000.

Screenshots from players in other countries are handy for spotting where a menu lives in the Play Store, though you shouldn't expect the exact same game coupons, point multipliers, or weekly perks on your own account.

Before using a calculator

  1. Open Google Play on the Google Account you'll actually use at checkout.
  2. Confirm its Play country and store currency.
  3. Note the current tier and base earn rate shown inside Play Points.
  4. Factor in only the bonus multiplier offers visible in your own Earn tab.
  5. Double-check Points history after your purchase clears.

FAQ

Does changing the phone language change the Play country?

No—changing your Android system language only translates menus and app text. Google Play decides your Play Points currency, thresholds, and offers strictly from the Play country and payment profile attached to your purchasing Google Account.

Can I use another country’s threshold or promotion?

No—thresholds, multipliers, and reward catalogs are locked to each account's Play country, so you can't claim a Japan or Korea promo on a US profile. Use screenshots or guides from another region only to spot where a setting sits, and always run your math with your own region's calculator.

What should I check before changing the Play country?

Review the country-change guide before touching anything in settings, because switching Play countries wipes out existing Play Points and tier progress and leaves old Play balances tied to the previous region. You'll also want to check active subscriptions, family group status, and local payment methods first.
