How much are 100 Google Play Points worth in the US?

Updated 2026-08-03 ・ US Play Points guide

This page is a US account example. Its dollar amounts, level earning rates, and official links use United States conditions. English-speaking users in another country should use the method, not copy the numbers: enter the currency, level, missing points, and earning rate shown in the local Google Play account.

Short answer for US accounts

At the US Bronze base rate of 1 point per $1, earning 100 points takes about $100 of eligible pre-tax spending. A promotion or higher US level can reduce that estimate.

100 points are not cash. Their use value depends on rewards actually shown on the account.

Spending needed at each US level

Using the cited US base rates, the rough eligible spend for 100 points is: Bronze $100 at 1 point per $1; Silver about $90.91 at 1.1; Gold about $83.34 at 1.2; Platinum about $71.43 at 1.4; Diamond $62.50 at 1.6.

Google calculates each eligible item and rounds to the nearest whole point. Taxes are excluded, so several purchases can differ from one division of the total.

Why redemption value is not fixed

Play Points can be used for in-app items, coupons, Play credit, or partner offers when available. Catalogs and required points differ by country, time, and account.

Check the Use tab, restrictions, minimum purchase, and expiration before treating a reward as savings.

How US promotions change the estimate

At US Bronze, an eligible 2x offer can reduce the rough spend from $100 to $50, and a 3x offer to about $33.34. That calculation applies only when the specific transaction qualifies.

Start at the normal rate, then check activation, dates, apps, caps, and account eligibility.

What non-US users should do

Do not convert the dollar table with an exchange rate and assume the result is a local Play Points requirement. Countries can have different thresholds, earning rates, taxes, rewards, and promotions.

Use the general points-cost calculator with the values shown on the purchasing account.

FAQ

Are 100 points always worth $1?

No. There is no universal cash rate.

How much do I spend for 100 points in the US?

About $100 at US Bronze before promotions and excluding tax.

Can I use this table outside the US?

Use only the calculation method and enter the local account values.

Can points be cashed out?

No.
