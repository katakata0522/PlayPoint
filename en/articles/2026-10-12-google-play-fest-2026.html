Google Play Fest 2026 in the US: October 13–19, sweepstakes and points boosts

Language: English · US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-14 Date details

Published October 12, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · Official info checked October 14, 2026

Saved articles & reading history

Play Fest is live, with the main event advertised for October 13–19, 2026. We checked the US page again on October 14: sweepstakes entries, daily Happy Hour boosts and book offers are worth looking at before a planned purchase. They have different conditions and deadlines, so treat each offer separately.

Start with the US sweepstakes

US Play Points members can exchange points for a chance to win prizes, including an Oscar Piastri replica racing helmet, Razer gaming gear and XREAL AURA glasses. An entry is a chance to win, not a guaranteed item exchanged for a fixed number of points.

Google’s official announcement says no purchase is necessary, participation is limited to selected US Play Points members aged 18 or older, and the sweepstakes ends November 2. That is a different deadline from the October 19 end of the main festival offers.

The linked Official Rules returned a 401 error in our October 14 check. Entry-bundle costs, state-specific requirements, the exact cutoff time and free-entry instructions remain unverified. Google’s August–September summer-sweepstakes Help page describes a different campaign, so its entry prices and mailing instructions should not be reused here.

There is more to try than shopping

The announcement names leagues for Angry Birds 2, Free Fire and 8 Ball Pool, plus Play Fest quests. If one of those games is already part of your routine, a league or quest may be the most enjoyable place to start.

The US event page now advertises 15x points on book purchases from October 16–18. It also says to claim a 12-hour Happy Hour boost at 3 PM each day. The public heading does not name a time zone or establish that Happy Hour always uses the Play Pass 6x rate; check the card’s eligible purchases, activation and expiry before paying.

Play Pass gets an early start

For Play Pass subscribers, the US preview announces double points and 6x boosts from October 8 through Play Fest. Those labels do not combine into a 12x purchase rate. The separate US Play Pass offer guide explains the weekly benefit, booster and activation checks.

Make room for a useful offer

Check which Google account and Play country you use, then compare a purchase you were already planning with the actual boost card. For sweepstakes entries, wait until you can read the applicable rules and points cost before making an exchange.

This is a good week to explore what your existing points can unlock. A reward you will enjoy is a better target than extra spending just to participate.
