Google Play Fest 2026 recap: US sweepstakes and past offers

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-11-06 Date details

Published October 12, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026 · Official info checked October 16, 2026

Saved articles & reading history

Google’s main Play Fest 2026 event wrapped up on October 19, 2026, and the US sweepstakes closed on November 2, 2026. Here is a clean archive of the US offers we logged during the event—including the sweepstakes entry bundles, daily Happy Hour boosts, book points promos, and Play Pass perks—so you can see how Google structured the week.

How the US sweepstakes worked

Eligible US Play Points members could trade points for sweepstakes entries toward prizes like an Oscar Piastri replica racing helmet, Razer gaming gear, and XREAL AURA glasses. Each trade bought entries into a drawing rather than a guaranteed prize at a set point price.

Under Google’s Official Rules, entrants needed to be legal residents of the 50 US states or DC and at least 18 when the window opened (19+ in Alabama and Nebraska; 21+ in Mississippi). US territories, military installations, and organizer-affiliated individuals were excluded.

Entry window: October 13, 2026 at 12:00 AM through November 2, 2026 at 11:59 PM PDT—two weeks longer than the main October 19 festival cutoff.

Bundle bonuses applied per single exchange, so you could not combine smaller trades to reach a higher bonus tier. Spent points were non-refundable whether you won or not. In the Android Play Store, entries lived under Play Points → You or Perks (with shipping info required), limited to one prize per account, and your Play Points tier did not boost your odds.

No purchase was required. A free mail-in option granted 50 entries per handwritten 3×5-inch card sent in a separate #10 envelope with first-class postage, postmarked October 13–November 2 and received by November 5. Full mailing instructions were set out in the Official Rules’ required details, address and mailing conditions.

Leagues, quests, Happy Hour, and book offers

Play Fest wasn’t just about spending points or buying packs. Google’s announcement highlighted in-game leagues for Angry Birds 2, Free Fire, and 8 Ball Pool alongside Play Fest quests, giving regular players extra milestones to chase during the week.

On the purchase side, the US event hub ran 15x points on book purchases from October 16–18 (now ended) plus a daily 12-hour Happy Hour boost starting at 3 PM. Because the public banner didn’t spell out a time zone or guarantee one fixed multiplier for every Happy Hour, players had to tap their own card to see eligible titles, activation steps, and expiration timers before buying.

Play Pass bonuses and early offers

The US event page checked on October 16 highlighted 2x weekly Play Pass bonuses (40 points) plus a 6x purchase boost. That weekly grant and purchase booster were separate perks—not every account automatically qualified, and they didn’t stack into a 12x rate. Our US Play Pass offer guide walks through how those two benefits worked and how activation rules applied.

How to approach future Play Fest promos

Keep these numbers as a historical snapshot rather than a guarantee of what next year’s promos will pay. Whenever a big Play Points event returns, confirm which Google account and Play country you are logged into, check the live multiplier card on your own screen, and save your points or spending for items you were planning to pick up anyway.
