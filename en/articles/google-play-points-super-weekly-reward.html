Google Play Points Super Weekly Prize: eligibility, prizes and Super Tickets

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-14 Date details

Published August 1, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · Official info checked October 2, 2026 · Play Points guide

Saved articles & reading history
The Super Weekly Prize gives eligible Gold, Platinum, and Diamond members a bigger weekly draw on Fridays, with Super Tickets offering a reroll option if you want another shot at a prize. Because availability, prize inventory, and extra rules vary by account and Play country, start with the card in your Perks tab before counting on a specific reward.

How it differs from the regular weekly prize

The names sound almost identical, so it's easy to mix them up—but they're separate perks. Google's regular weekly prize starts at Silver (150 annual points in the US) and refreshes on Fridays. The Super Weekly Prize promotion targets Gold (600 points), Platinum (3,000 points), and Diamond (10,000 points), and may include extra account-specific requirements. Meanwhile, Play Pass weekly bonuses are a separate Thursday perk in supported regions.

Before assuming something is broken, check which card you were expecting to see: the regular Friday prize, the Super Weekly Prize, or your Thursday Play Pass bonus.

Eligibility, Friday refresh timing, and limited stock

When the Super Weekly Prize is running on your account, Google says new prizes open up after 12:00 a.m. local time each Friday. What's inside the prize pool depends on your tier, and individual prizes have limited stock that can run out.

Reaching Gold or higher doesn't automatically guarantee every promo item every week—read the Perks card in Google Play for your current eligibility, remaining inventory, and terms.

  • Gold, Platinum, and Diamond are the tiers listed on the promotion.
  • Some accounts may see extra eligibility requirements right on the promo card.
  • Prize quantities are limited, and high-ticket rewards are much rarer than point drops.
  • Past hardware, gift card, and bonus-point prizes show the kind of rewards that have appeared before, not what's guaranteed in stock this week.

When budgeting toward a tier, treat the reward as an uncertain benefit rather than counting a fixed dollar value toward your goal.

How to claim it without mixing up accounts

  1. Open Google Play and verify you're signed into the Google account enrolled in Play Points.
  2. Confirm your current tier and US Play country setting.
  3. Go to Play Points → Perks after midnight local time on Friday.
  4. Check the card's eligibility, stock notes, and claim deadline before tapping.
  5. Claim the reward, then check Points history if you aren't sure whether the points credited.

If you juggle multiple Google accounts on the same Android phone (for example, a main gaming account and a work Gmail), tap your profile icon first—tier perks on one account won't appear when another account is active.

Super Tickets: eligibility, deadlines, and reroll trade-offs

Google's US level help publishes specific rules for Super Tickets, which are available in Japan, Korea, Taiwan, the United Kingdom, and the United States:

  • Weekly drops: Platinum and Diamond members receive one ticket each Tuesday. Play Pass subscribers at Gold, Platinum, or Diamond receive an additional ticket each Thursday.
  • 48-hour save window: Save your ticket within 48 hours after it becomes available—if you miss that window, the unsaved ticket disappears.
  • Eight-week expiry: A saved ticket expires on the first Thursday after eight weeks from when it became available, not from the day you saved it.
  • Reroll trade-off: Choosing Try again gives up the first prize completely, and the replacement reward can be lower or higher. If you already pulled a solid point drop or voucher, tap Keep prize instead of gambling it away.

Always review the current card on your account for live availability and prize stock. These rules do not guarantee a particular prize or any fixed monetary return.

What to check when the card doesn't show up

  1. You're viewing the Google account that holds your Gold, Platinum, or Diamond tier.
  2. Your Play country matches a supported region where the promo is active.
  3. It's already past 12:00 a.m. local time on Friday.
  4. You haven't already claimed this week's prize earlier in the day.
  5. There isn't an out-of-stock notice or extra eligibility step on the Perks card.
  6. You aren't looking for the regular Silver-and-up prize or the Thursday Play Pass perk instead.

PlayPoint can't inspect live account eligibility, ticket drops, or prize inventory. If a card is visible on your phone but won't let you claim as described, grab a private screenshot of the card terms and contact Google Play support directly (avoid posting order numbers or account details in public forums).

FAQ

Can Silver members claim the Super Weekly Prize?

No—the Super Weekly Prize promotion is listed for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond members (starting at 600 annual points in the US). If you're at Silver (150+ points), you still get the separate regular Friday weekly prize in your Perks tab. Once you cross into Gold, Super Weekly eligibility unlocks alongside your regular tier benefits.

Does claiming early guarantee a better reward?

Not necessarily. While popular physical or limited-stock prizes can run out if inventory is claimed, Google doesn't publish draw odds and no particular premium item is guaranteed just for tapping right after midnight. Claiming whenever it fits your Friday routine works fine for most players.

How should I include this perk in a purchase decision?

Base your spending decisions on games, passes, and in-app items you already planned to buy, then compare the steady tier earn rates (1.2 to 1.6 points per $1 from Gold to Diamond) on their own merits. Because prize odds and future stock aren't published, the Super Weekly Prize shouldn't be treated as guaranteed cash value that “pays back” extra spending.
