Google Play Points Super Weekly Prize: eligibility, prizes and Super Tickets

Updated 2026-08-05 ・ Play Points guide

Use this guide only when the Super Weekly Prize card is currently shown for your Play country and Google account. Google lists Gold, Platinum and Diamond members for the promotion, but availability and extra requirements can still differ by region and account. The card on your own Perks screen is the practical starting point.

How it differs from the regular weekly prize

The names are similar, but they are separate benefits. Google's regular weekly prize starts at Silver. The Super Weekly Prize promotion lists Gold, Platinum and Diamond and may add account-specific eligibility. Play Pass weekly bonuses are another program that refreshes on Thursday in supported regions.

Before troubleshooting, identify which screen you expected: the regular weekly prize, the Super Weekly Prize, or a Play Pass weekly bonus.

Eligibility, timing and limited stock

When the promotion is available, Google says new prizes become available after 12:00 a.m. local time on Friday. Prize sets can differ by level, and quantities can run out.

A qualifying level alone does not prove that the promotion is active for every country or account. Read the card shown in Google Play for the current eligibility, remaining stock and terms.

  • Gold, Platinum and Diamond are the levels listed on the promotion.
  • Additional requirements may be displayed for an individual account.
  • Prize quantities are limited and some rewards are rarer than others.
  • Past hardware, gift card and points examples describe the format, not this week's inventory.

For purchase planning, treat the reward as an uncertain benefit rather than adding a fixed prize value to the level.

How to claim it without mixing up accounts

  1. Open Google Play with the account enrolled in Play Points.
  2. Confirm the current level and Play country.
  3. Open Play Points and the Perks area after the local Friday refresh.
  4. Read the displayed eligibility, prize stock and additional terms.
  5. Claim the reward, then use Points history if you are unsure whether it completed.

With several Google accounts, check the account icon before comparing the level and reward screen. A level on one account does not unlock a card on another.

Super Tickets: compare before using one

A Super Ticket gives another chance at a premium reward after you claim a Play Points reward from the Super Weekly Prize. The key condition is that you do not keep the previous reward when you use a ticket.

Google currently states that Play Pass subscribers and Diamond members receive one ticket every 30 days, while Platinum members receive one every 90 days. The actual ticket and claim window still need to appear on your account.

It is a replacement decision, not an extra reward

Compare the reward already shown with the uncertain result of another draw. A useful first reward may be worth keeping; a ticket can also be saved for a later eligible draw.

What to check when it does not appear

  1. The Google account enrolled in Play Points
  2. The current level and Play country
  3. Whether the local Friday refresh has passed
  4. Whether this week's reward was already claimed
  5. Any additional eligibility or out-of-stock message
  6. Whether you were looking for the regular or Play Pass reward instead

This site cannot see live eligibility, ticket inventory or prize stock. When the card is displayed but does not work as described, keep a private screenshot of its terms and contact Google Play support without posting account or order details publicly.

FAQ

Can Silver members claim the Super Weekly Prize?

The Super Weekly Prize promotion lists Gold, Platinum and Diamond. Silver members may still have the separate regular weekly prize.

Does claiming early guarantee a better reward?

No. Limited stock can change, but no particular premium item is guaranteed.

How should I include this perk in a purchase decision?

Start with purchases you already plan to make and compare the level benefits separately. Because prize probabilities and future stock are not published, the Super Weekly Prize should not be counted as guaranteed monetary value.
