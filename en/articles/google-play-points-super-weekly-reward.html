Google Play Points Super Weekly Prize: eligibility, prizes and Super Tickets

Updated 2026-08-02 ・ Play Points guide

The Super Weekly Prize is a separate, stock-limited weekly perk for eligible Gold, Platinum and Diamond members. New rewards become available after local midnight on Friday. It is not a promise of a premium device or gift card, so check the reward screen before treating screenshots from another account as your likely outcome.

How it differs from the regular weekly prize

The names are similar, but the eligibility rules are not. Google's regular weekly prize guide starts at Silver. The Super Weekly Prize promotion lists Gold, Platinum and Diamond and may apply extra eligibility requirements.

Play Pass weekly bonuses are another separate benefit. Before troubleshooting, identify which of the three screens you expected to see: the regular weekly prize, the Super Weekly Prize, or a Play Pass weekly benefit.

Eligibility, timing and limited stock

Google's promotion page says new prizes are available after 12:00 a.m. local time on Friday. Prize sets can differ by level, and the page can show remaining quantities before you claim if your account is eligible.

Availability varies by Play country and account. If the official promotion page or perk does not appear, do not assume the Super Weekly Prize is offered in your region.

  • Gold, Platinum and Diamond are the listed levels.
  • Additional eligibility rules may appear on your account.
  • Prize quantities are limited and can run out.
  • Some rewards are rarer than others.
  • Past Pixel devices, watches, gift cards and gaming gear are examples, not a current guarantee.

This is why an old prize list is useful for understanding the format but poor evidence for deciding how much to spend today.

How to claim it without mixing up accounts

  1. Open Google Play with the account enrolled in Play Points.
  2. Confirm that the current level is Gold or above.
  3. Open Play Points and check the Perks area after the local Friday refresh.
  4. Read the displayed eligibility, prize stock and additional terms.
  5. Claim the reward, then check Points history if you are unsure whether it completed.

When you use several Google accounts, the most common mistake is checking the level on one account and opening the reward screen on another. Switch accounts first instead of repeatedly refreshing the app.

Super Tickets: the part that needs a decision

A Super Ticket gives another chance at a premium reward after you claim a Play Points reward from the Super Weekly Prize. The important detail is easy to miss: when you use a Super Ticket, you do not keep the previous reward.

Google currently says Play Pass subscribers and Diamond members receive one ticket every 30 days, while Platinum members receive one every 90 days. Availability can still depend on the promotion shown to your account.

Do not tap through automatically

Compare the reward already shown with the uncertain chance of a premium reward. If the first reward is useful to you, using a ticket is not a free extra draw; it replaces what you had.

What to check when it does not appear

  1. The Google account enrolled in Play Points
  2. The current level and any recent level change
  3. The Play country and local Friday date
  4. Whether this week's reward was already claimed
  5. Any additional eligibility or out-of-stock message
  6. Whether you were actually looking for the regular or Play Pass reward

The site cannot see your current reward inventory or eligibility. If the account meets the displayed rules but the screen remains unavailable, keep a private screenshot of the terms and contact Google Play support. Do not post order numbers or account details publicly.

FAQ

Can Silver members claim the Super Weekly Prize?

Google's Super Weekly Prize promotion lists Gold, Platinum and Diamond. Silver is covered by the separate regular weekly prize guide.

Is claiming earlier guaranteed to produce a better reward?

No. Google says claiming earlier can improve the chance while premium stock remains, but it does not guarantee a particular item.

Should I spend extra just to reach Gold?

Not based on the reward alone. The probability and expected value of premium prizes are not published, so use spending you already planned as the baseline.

Keep the weekly reward separate from your spending plan

Use the calculator for purchases you already expect to make. Treat a weekly reward as an uncertain bonus rather than guaranteed value.
