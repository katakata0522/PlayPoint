Which purchases earn Google Play Points? Apps, games, books and subscriptions

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Answer: eligible Google Play purchases can earn Play Points

For US Play Points accounts, eligible paid apps, games, in-app or in-game items, subscriptions, and Google Play books all earn points. The key rule is simple: the purchase must go through Google Play while you're signed into your enrolled Google Account.

Sales tax never counts toward points, and eligible content varies by Play country, so the estimate shown right on your Google Play checkout sheet is the best quick check before you tap pay.

Paid apps and full games

Buying a paid app or full game from Google Play earns Play Points at your current tier rate, as long as the item qualifies and you check out with your enrolled account. Simply downloading a free app or free-to-play game won't give you points on its own.

The one exception is when Google runs a featured install offer or quest in your Play Points Earn tab. In that case, you'll need to tap into the offer first and follow the exact install or play requirement listed there—normal store listings don't trigger those bonus points automatically.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

In-game currency packs, battle passes, and eligible recurring subscriptions earn points whenever the billing goes through Google Play's checkout sheet. Buying the same pack through a game publisher's web shop or another outside payment system won't count as a Google Play transaction.

Before confirming a top-up or subscription, glance at the Google Play payment dialog to verify the right account is selected, check that any 2x or 3x earn boost is already activated, and review your Points history afterward if a bonus looks off.

Google Play Books and monthly book perks

Yes, books count in the US—Google's official US earning rules include eligible Google Play Books purchases, and the US level table even includes monthly book point multipliers as a tier benefit.

That said, book promos aren't static across every title or month. Check the Play Points Earn tab and the checkout preview on the book you want before buying to see whether a multiplier applies right now.

How sales tax, discounts, and rounding affect your total

Google calculates points from the eligible pre-tax item price, not the final total with sales tax added. That pre-tax amount is multiplied by your active rate (from 1 point per $1 at Bronze up to 1.6 points per $1 at Diamond, or a higher promo rate) and rounded to the nearest whole point.

Because rounding happens on each individual purchase—and any promo discount lowers the eligible price—buying several small $0.99 items separately can leave your Points history slightly different from multiplying your monthly spend by your tier rate.

Buying on PC, tablet, or Smart TV

You don't have to buy on an Android phone to earn points. Eligible Google Play purchases made on tablets, computers, Smart TVs, or through Google Play Games on PC earn Play Points just the same, provided you're signed into your enrolled account and paying via Google Play billing.

Just don't confuse Play Games XP or achievements with Play Points—only qualifying purchases, install offers, or active quests add to your points balance.

Quick pre-purchase checklist

  • Check the profile icon to confirm you're using the account enrolled in US Play Points.
  • Make sure the checkout screen is Google Play's billing dialog, not an external web store.
  • Activate any limited-time multiplier or install promo in the Earn tab before buying.
  • Leave sales tax out when estimating points yourself.
  • Compare the checkout preview against Points history once the order clears.

If an eligible order settles and points still don't show up, verify your enrollment date, check that the order isn't still pending, and double-check which Google Account actually got the receipt before reaching out to support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do book purchases earn Play Points?

Yes—in the US, eligible Google Play Books purchases earn Play Points right alongside paid apps, games, and in-app items. As long as you buy through Google Play on your enrolled account, your tier rate applies to the pre-tax price, and US levels also feature monthly book multipliers in the Earn tab. Check the checkout screen before paying to see the exact points estimate for your title.
