Pokémon Sleep Premium Pass: one month or six months? Compare the daily benefits

Language: English · US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Published 2026-10-14 Date details

Published October 14, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · Official info checked October 14, 2026 · Play Points guide

Saved articles & reading history
If checking new Pokémon sleep styles has become part of your morning, the Premium Pass may be tempting. One month gives you a way to try it; six months deserves a closer look when you already want to keep playing. Start with what you will use each day.

The daily bonus adds up

An active Premium Pass gives a bonus 100 sleep points each day you track sleep. Tracking on 30 eligible days gives 3,000 bonus sleep points. The Premium Exchange and upgraded daily biscuit also make those regular check-ins more interesting.

Compare the prices on your own store

Read both the one-month and six-month prices in your app. Compare monthly price × 6 with the six-month price, then divide the six-month price by 6 for its monthly equivalent. Use the same country, platform and tax basis; Japanese yen prices are not a US quote.

Match the duration to your routine

If you are still finding a sleep-tracking routine, a shorter plan may be easier to judge. If you expect to use the benefits for six months, compare the longer plan’s total saving with the chance that you will stop using it earlier.

Check your subscription streak before switching

Pokémon’s billing FAQ explains that Android plan changes can require cancellation, expiry and purchasing the new plan, which resets the streak. Changing stores also resets it. Check those steps before changing a plan simply because its monthly equivalent looks lower.

Sleep points and Google Play Points are separate

The daily bonus in this article is Pokémon Sleep’s in-game currency. Google Play Points concerns eligible purchases billed through Google Play. Keep the two balances separate when assessing the pass.

Choose the benefits you will enjoy

Write down the daily features you use and the number of months you realistically expect to keep playing. That gives the price comparison a purpose beyond choosing the longest plan.

Check the numbers without forcing a purchase

Use the calculator as a planning estimate, then confirm the official rule in your local Google Play app.

FAQ

Does the pass’s daily bonus add to Google Play Points?

No. The bonus is sleep points inside Pokémon Sleep.

Can I use Japan’s listed price in a US comparison?

No. Use the current prices displayed for your country and purchase platform.
