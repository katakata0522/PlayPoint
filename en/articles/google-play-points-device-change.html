Google Play Points after changing phones or devices

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Answer: the same Google account keeps your Points

Your Google Play Points live on your Google account and Play country—they aren't stored locally on your old phone. Upgrading phones or resetting a device doesn't wipe your balance, tier status, or point history once you sign back into the same Google account.

If your new phone shows a zero balance or no Play Points menu at all, you're almost always looking at a different Gmail profile or country setting rather than lost points.

Match what's on your screen first

  • The Play Points menu is missing: check which Google account is active in the Play Store, its Play country, and whether the program is supported there.
  • The menu is there, but the balance reads zero: double-check the profile email and peek at point history to see if you're on a secondary account.
  • Your tier looks different than before: make sure you didn't accidentally flip between two Google accounts or change Play countries.
  • Only one new purchase is missing: verify which account got the email receipt and whether the order is still pending before blaming the phone swap.

Pinpointing the exact symptom saves you from signing out of everything on your phone or touching settings that have nothing to do with the issue.

Check your active Google account in order

  1. Open the Google Play Store app and tap your profile picture in the top right.
  2. Read the full Gmail address currently selected at the top of the menu.
  3. Switch to the exact Google account you originally used to join Play Points.
  4. Open Play Points, then tap More → Points history.
  5. If only a single recent order is missing, check which email received that Google Play receipt.

When you set up a new phone, it's easy to add a work or secondary Gmail first—and Android sometimes defaults Google Play to that profile. Remember that multiple Google accounts on one device keep separate balances that can never be merged, and points always go to the account that completed the payment.

Don't change your Play country as a quick fix

Swapping phones is completely different from changing your Play country. Google explicitly warns that changing your Play country wipes out your previous country's Points, tier progress, and unused rewards—and can disrupt payment methods and app availability too.

Never tap into country settings just to troubleshoot a new phone. Only switch your Play country if you've genuinely moved to a new country and understand everything that resets.

When to wait and when to message support

Right after setting up a phone, Play Store menus or a brand-new purchase can take a moment to sync. Restart the Google Play Store app, make sure it's updated, and check point history again before buying anything twice (Play Pass weekly perks can also have a separate confirmation window).

If you're signed into the right account and country, the order has fully settled, and point history still skips it, contact Google Play support with your order ID, purchase date, amount, account email, and screenshots—and keep those details out of public posts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Play Points transfer when switching devices?

Yes—your Play Points balance, tier level, and point history are tied to your Google account rather than your phone, so signing into the same Google account on a new device brings everything over automatically. If your points don't appear right after setup, tap your Play Store profile icon to make sure Android didn't switch you onto a secondary Gmail profile.
