New phone and missing Points

Google Play Points after changing phones or devices

Published August 4, 2026 · Updated August 4, 2026 · Official info checked August 4, 2026 · 6 min read

Answer: the same Google account normally keeps your Points

Google Play Points are associated with the Google account and Play country used for the program, not stored only on one phone. A normal device replacement should not erase the balance, level or point history when you sign in to the same account.

When the new phone shows zero Points or no Play Points menu, first suspect an account or country mismatch rather than a lost balance.

Choose the symptom that matches your screen

  • The Play Points menu is missing: check the active Google account, Play country and program availability.
  • The menu exists but the balance is zero: compare the profile email and open point history.
  • The level looks different: confirm that you did not switch accounts or Play countries.
  • A recent purchase is missing: check the purchase account, order status and the point history before blaming the device change.

This symptom-first approach avoids signing out of every account or changing settings that are unrelated to the actual problem.

Check the account in this order

  1. Open Google Play and tap the profile image.
  2. Read the full email address of the current account.
  3. Switch to the account that originally joined Play Points.
  4. Open Play Points and then point history.
  5. Compare the purchase receipt account if only one transaction is missing.

Multiple Google accounts can be present on one phone, but their Play Points cannot be combined or moved. The account that made a purchase receives the eligible Points.

Do not change the Play country as a quick fix

A device replacement and a Play country change are different events. Google warns that changing the Play country can cause the previous country’s Points, level and unused rewards to be lost. It can also affect payment methods and available content.

Only change the Play country when you actually meet Google’s country-change conditions and understand the consequences. It should not be the first troubleshooting step for a new phone.

When to wait and when to contact support

Some account benefits or recent transactions can take time to appear on another device. Restart Google Play, update the app and check the history again before repeating purchases. A Play Pass benefit may also have a separate confirmation period.

Contact Google Play support when the same account and country show a verified completed purchase but the corresponding history remains missing. Prepare the order ID, date, amount, account email and screenshots; do not publish those details publicly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Play Points transfer when switching devices?

Yes. Play Points are tied to your Google account, so logging into the same account transfers your points automatically.
