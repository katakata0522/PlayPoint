Colorful+ and MYSEKAI passes: choose the rewards you want

What to choose

Choose by the reward you want: Colorful+ is the Crystal subscription; World Pass and MYSEKAI Mission Pass serve different MYSEKAI goals. Within Colorful+, compare Basic, Standard and Deluxe using both the paid/free Crystal split and the login rewards you will collect. The highest tier is useful only if you want its added contents.

Start with the product

Colorful+ Basic / Standard / Deluxe

Each tier has paid and free Crystals, items and a 14-day login bonus. First-month discounts change the Crystal quantities; compare tiers before choosing.

World Pass / MYSEKAI Mission Pass

The English version’s 5.0 update introduced these MYSEKAI-related products. They are distinct from Colorful+ and its Crystal subscription.

A subscription and a mission pass are different purchases

SEGA’s Colorful+ FAQ separates paid and free Crystals and a 14-day login bonus for each tier. First-month discounts change the Crystal quantities, so compare the first purchase with the first purchase and later months with later months.

World Pass and MYSEKAI Mission Pass arrived with the English 5.0 update. Keep their MYSEKAI rewards separate from the Colorful+ Crystal comparison instead of merging all three into a generic “monthly pass.”

Put your own budget into the comparison

List the payment amount, what you receive now and what you will receive later. Compare the rewards you will use before your own deadline. Then estimate points on the amount you plan to pay, rather than increasing the purchase just to earn more points.

Read the current Android offer for the amount you will pay. Product listings from another store establish names or contents, not the Google Play price.

Where Play Points fit

Eligible Google Play purchases earn Play Points. Spending game currency you already own does not create another purchase, and an official web checkout is billed separately. Use the final earn rate shown on your Google account; future points are a separate benefit from the game resources delivered today.

FAQ

Are Colorful+ and MYSEKAI Mission Pass the same subscription?

No. Colorful+ focuses on Crystals and its tier benefits; the MYSEKAI products have different contents and goals.
