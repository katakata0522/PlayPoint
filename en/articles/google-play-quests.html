Google Play quest not working? Check progress and purchase rules

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-10-14 Date details

Published August 1, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · Official info checked October 14, 2026 · Google Play guide

Saved articles & reading history
A Google Play quest is an offer that rewards you for completing all of its required steps. The offer, deadline and task list can differ by account. Use your own quest progress page as the final rule instead of copying another user's purchase amount or play condition.

Quest missing or stuck? Start with the type of failure

What you seeCheck first
The quest never appearsEligibility is account-specific; another user seeing it does not mean your account should.
A purchase step does not countThe transaction must earn Play Points; redeeming points or paying with points does not count as a quest purchase.
A game/play step stays incompleteCheck the Google account, Play Games profile and any Play personalization requirement shown by the quest.
A completed quest becomes incompleteA refund of a qualifying purchase returns the quest to an incomplete state; a claimed reward may also be reversed.
Progress is still missing after the stated processing timeSave the quest terms, order time and account used before contacting Google Play support.

Do not make another purchase just to test the quest until you know which condition failed. The quest progress page on your own account is the final rule for steps, deadline and reward.

Quest progress not updating? Check one unfinished step at a time

Open the quest on your phone and select “See Quest” or its progress page. Find the step still marked incomplete: a purchase, a game activity or the final completion requirement. Finishing one step does not automatically finish the others.

  1. Purchase step: compare the order with the amount, item and transaction count on your own quest. Check whether that transaction earned Play Points. A pending, canceled or refunded order needs a different check from a completed qualifying order.
  2. Game step: match the Google account and Play Games profile, then review the play-time or achievement condition. Use Play personalization only if your quest requires it and you choose to accept that setting.
  3. Still stuck: follow any processing window stated by the offer. Save the unfinished step, deadline and transaction details for Google Play support rather than buying the same item again as a test.

For example, if both a purchase and a game task are required, a checked purchase box tells you to investigate the game task next. It does not show that a second payment would help. This is a way to read the progress screen, not a promise about a particular offer.

Sources: Google’s quest conditions and Play Points troubleshooting.

Where quests appear and why yours may be missing

Use the Google Play Store on your phone. Google says quests and stamp cards are currently unavailable in Google Play Games on PC and Google Play on the web. A quest missing on your computer is not evidence that your phone offer has disappeared. Supported devices.

Eligible users may see a quest on the Play Points page or in Play Store tabs such as Games, Apps or You. Not seeing an offer that appears on somebody else's account is not, by itself, evidence of an error.

Google says the quests you receive can depend on account settings or activity, including Play Points level where relevant. The steps and rewards may also differ between users. (Google’s official guidance)

Five details to save before you start

  1. Deadline: check both the completion deadline and any reward claim deadline.
  2. Account: confirm the Google account that opened or started the quest.
  3. Exact steps: save the app, game, amount, count and play requirements.
  4. Order: steps are normally flexible unless the quest page says otherwise.
  5. Settings: note whether Play personalization or a Play Games profile is required.

A screenshot before purchase is more useful than trying to reconstruct the terms after progress fails. Keep it private because account names and offer details may be visible.

For a hypothetical US offer requiring two purchases of at least $5 each, one $10 purchase would still be only one purchase. A completed purchase step would not finish a separate play-time step either. This is an example of how to read a task, not a currently available offer; use the amounts and task count on your own screen.

What counts as a purchase

For a Play Points reward quest, Google defines a purchase as a Google Play transaction that earns Play Points. Depending on the country and offer, this can include:

  • paid apps or games;
  • in-app or in-game items;
  • subscription payments and renewals;
  • book or movie rentals that earn points in the region.

Redeeming points for an item, or paying with points, does not count. Those actions do not generate new Play Points. An extended trial without a payment earns no points and does not meet the purchase requirement.

Play Games sign-in and personalization

Some game-related quests require Play personalization so Google Play can track in-game activity. Some also require the game session to use a signed-in Play Games profile.

  1. Check the Google account used for the quest.
  2. Check the account signed in to the game and Play Games profile.
  3. Read the quest page for a personalization requirement.
  4. Confirm the required play time, achievement or task is actually complete.
  5. Reopen the progress page and look for a stated processing period.

Do not turn on additional tracking just because a third-party guide says so. Read the condition on your quest and make your own privacy choice.

Cancellations, refunds and missing progress

If a qualifying purchase is canceled while the quest is incomplete, Google says you can make another qualifying purchase while the offer remains active. If a qualifying purchase is refunded after you complete the quest, the quest returns to an incomplete state. You can complete it again while the offer is still active.

  • A claimed quest reward may be reversed when the purchase affecting eligibility is reversed.
  • If a purchase is refunded or a task is canceled after an optional bonus task is complete, that bonus task does not return to an incomplete state.
  • Pending or canceled orders should be checked before assuming a tracking failure.

When progress still does not update, record the quest name, deadline, step, order time and the account used. Contact Google Play support after the stated processing time. Never publish an order number or email address in a forum.

FAQ

Can I complete the steps in any order?

Usually yes, unless the quest progress page gives a specific order.

Does a Play Pass renewal count?

Google lists a Play Pass subscription renewal as a purchase when it earns Play Points and meets the quest terms.

Why did my completed quest become incomplete?

A refund of a qualifying purchase after completion returns the quest to an incomplete state. If you already claimed the reward, reversing a purchase that affects the quest may also reverse that reward.

Only calculate spending after confirming the task

If a purchase you already planned meets the quest terms, use the calculator to estimate the points earned at your account’s displayed rate. The calculator does not check quest eligibility or calculate the quest reward. Do not buy first and search for the quest later.
