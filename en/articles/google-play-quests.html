Google Play quest not working? Check progress and purchase rules

US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

Updated 2026-11-05 Date details

Published August 1, 2026 · Updated November 5, 2026 · Official info checked November 5, 2026 · Google Play guide

Saved articles & reading history
A Google Play quest rewards you only after you finish all of its required steps before the deadline. Because quest tasks, eligible spend, and deadlines are tailored per account, start from the progress screen in your own Google Play Store rather than copying someone else's purchase amount or play condition.

Hold off on making a second purchase just to “test” a stuck quest. Pinpoint which single requirement hasn't cleared first—most tracking snags come from a separate game task, a different signed-in account, or a non-qualifying redemption.

Quest progress not updating? Check one unfinished step at a time

Open the quest on your Android phone and tap See Quest to pull up its progress checklist. Multi-step quests track each requirement separately, so clearing a purchase step won't check off a gameplay step for you.

  1. Purchase step: match your Google Play receipt against the exact game, pre-tax minimum spend, and number of transactions your quest asks for—and verify in Points history that the order actually earned Play Points. A pending, canceled, or refunded charge won't count.
  2. Game step: confirm that the game and your Play Games profile are signed into the exact same Google account as the Play Store, then review the play-time or achievement goal. Turn on Play personalization only if the quest explicitly requires it and you're comfortable enabling it.
  3. Still stuck: wait through any processing delay listed on the quest page. If nothing updates, keep your unfinished step, deadline, and order details ready for Google Play support instead of buying the same pack twice.

For example, if a quest asks for both a $5 pack purchase and 15 minutes of gameplay, seeing the purchase box checked tells you to look at your Play Games profile or play time next—not to spend another $5.

Where quests appear and why yours may be missing

Check the Google Play Store app on your phone. Google notes that quests and stamp cards aren't available in Google Play Games on PC or Google Play on the web. Not seeing a quest on your computer doesn't mean you lost access on mobile. Supported devices.

When a quest is active for your account, it typically shows up on your Play Points tab or inside Play Store tabs like Games, Apps, or You. Because Google rolls out quests selectively based on account activity, settings, and Play Points tier, seeing someone else post a quest doesn't mean every account gets the same offer. (Google’s official guidance)

Five details to save before you start

  1. Deadlines: note both when the tasks must be finished and how long you have to claim the reward.
  2. Active Google account: verify which Gmail account opened the quest, especially if you have multiple profiles on one phone.
  3. Exact task math: save the required app or game, minimum purchase amount, number of buys, and gameplay condition.
  4. Task order: steps can usually be done in any order unless the quest card specifies a sequence.
  5. Tracking settings: check whether Play personalization or a Play Games profile sign-in is required.

Snapping a quick private screenshot before you spend saves a lot of guesswork if progress lags later (keep it off public forums since account details can show).

If your quest reward is an in-game item, open the game app within 72 hours after completing the quest to claim it. Google says unclaimed items expire after that window. Keep that 72-hour pickup clock in mind so you don't mix it up with the deadline for completing the quest steps themselves.

Watch the transaction count carefully: if a US quest asks for “two purchases of at least $5 each,” buying a single $10 bundle only checks off 1 of 2 purchases—and finishing the spending side won't auto-complete a separate play-time step. Always match the exact step count shown on your quest card.

What counts as a quest purchase

For a Play Points reward quest, Google only counts transactions processed through Google Play that actually earn Play Points. Depending on your Play country and the specific offer, qualifying purchases can include:

  • paid apps or games;
  • in-app or in-game items;
  • subscription payments and paid renewals;
  • book or movie rentals that earn Play Points in your region.

Redeeming Play Points for an item—or paying with points—does not count. Those exchanges don't generate new Play Points, and free trials with no billed charge don't satisfy a purchase step either.

Play Games sign-in and Play personalization

Whenever a quest asks you to play a game for a set amount of time or hit an in-game goal, Google Play typically needs two things lined up: an active Play Games profile inside the game, and Play personalization enabled so activity can sync back to your quest tracker.

  1. Confirm which Google account owns the quest in the Play Store.
  2. Check that the game and Play Games profile are signed into that same Google account.
  3. Look at the quest terms to see if Play personalization is listed as a requirement.
  4. Double-check that you met the full play-time, level, or achievement condition.
  5. Reopen the quest progress card and allow for any stated processing window.

Only toggle Play personalization on if the specific quest you want requires it and you're comfortable with that privacy setting.

Cancellations, refunds, and reversed progress

What happens if an order falls through? If a qualifying purchase is canceled while the quest is still active and unfinished, Google says you can make another qualifying purchase before the offer expires. If a qualifying purchase is refunded after you already completed the quest, the quest reverts to an incomplete state—and you can only re-complete it if the quest window hasn't closed yet.

  • If you already claimed the quest reward, refunding the purchase that unlocked it may reverse that reward too.
  • Optional bonus tasks behave differently: if a purchase is refunded or canceled after you already finished an optional bonus task, Google says that completed bonus task doesn't revert to incomplete.
  • Check Google Play order history for pending or canceled payments before assuming quest tracking broke.

If everything looks qualified and the processing window has passed with zero movement, collect the quest title, deadline, unfinished step, order timestamp, and Google account used, then contact Google Play support privately.

FAQ

Can I complete the steps in any order?

Usually yes—most quests let you tackle purchase steps and gameplay steps in whichever order works best for you. However, if your quest card explicitly numbers steps in sequence or says one task unlocks another, follow the order shown on that progress screen.

Does a Play Pass renewal count?

It can. Google lists a Play Pass subscription renewal as an eligible purchase whenever that renewal earns Play Points and meets the quest's terms. Free trial periods or renewals that aren't covered by the quest's specific app or spend rules won't count.

Why did my completed quest become incomplete?

That almost always happens after a refund or payment reversal on one of the purchases that qualified you for the quest. If you already claimed the quest points or reward, reversing the underlying purchase can also trigger a reversal of that quest reward. As long as the quest deadline hasn't passed, you can still make a new qualifying purchase to finish it again.
