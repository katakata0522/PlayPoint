Pokémon TCG Pocket Premium Pass: benefits, trial and cancellation

Language: English · US reference. Offers depend on your Google Play country and account.

What to choose

If you want an extra pack every day, start with the 14-day Premium Pass trial. The Google Play introduction describes one extra daily pack: 10 days of use means 10 extra openings, while 30 days means 30. Keep it only if those openings and the current premium missions are worth the monthly price to you.

Start with the product

Premium Pass

A monthly subscription for additional pack stamina and premium missions. It is tied to the store account used to subscribe; deleting the app does not cancel it.

How many extra packs will you actually open?

At one extra pack per day, 10 days gives 10 extra openings and 30 days gives 30. These are usage examples, not an immediate bundle of packs. For a pack-only comparison, divide the monthly price shown on Android by the extra openings you will use. Count premium mission items separately.

Google Play’s product introduction describes the extra daily pack. The current in-game Premium Pass screen lists the benefits for your account; use that screen when planning openings and claim missions before their own deadline.

What you get, and what you still need to claim

The pass adds pack stamina and access to premium missions. Promo cards and accessories come from mission rewards or the Premium shop, including exchanges using premium tickets. Look at the current lineup before subscribing for one particular item.

Mission and shop updates have their own schedule. Your renewal date does not freeze the lineup for a whole subscription month, so check the item’s deadline as well as your pass expiry.

Use the trial, then choose whether to renew

The no-cost trial lasts 14 days and is available once per Apple or Google account. After it ends, billing continues automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial expires. Cancellation stops the next renewal; the current benefits remain until expiry.

On Android, open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pokémon TCG Pocket. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription. Keep the subscribing Google account available when changing devices.

Put your own budget into the comparison

List the payment amount, what you receive now and what you will receive later. Compare the rewards you will use before your own deadline. Then estimate points on the amount you plan to pay, rather than increasing the purchase just to earn more points.

Read the current Android offer for the amount you will pay. Product listings from another store establish names or contents, not the Google Play price.

Where Play Points fit

Eligible Google Play purchases earn Play Points. Spending game currency you already own does not create another purchase, and an official web checkout is billed separately. Use the final earn rate shown on your Google account; future points are a separate benefit from the game resources delivered today.

FAQ

Can I get another trial with a new save?

Not on the same Apple or Google platform account according to official support.
