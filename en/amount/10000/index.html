FAQ

Is $50 before or after tax?

Use the eligible item price before tax. Google excludes tax from Play Points calculations in the US.

Can I use this outside the US?

Use your local-currency PlayPoint page and the earning rate shown for your country or account. This preset is for US dollars.

Do gift cards earn points?

Buying or redeeming a gift card does not by itself mean the card amount earns Play Points. Check how the eligible Google Play purchase is treated in your account.
