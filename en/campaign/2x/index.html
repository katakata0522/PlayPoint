How much does a Google Play Points 2x promotion help?

This legacy “2x” page models a final special earn rate of 2 points per $1 when Google Play shows that rate for an eligible purchase. It does not multiply your tier base rate by 2.

FAQ

Does 2x make the cost exactly half?

Not necessarily. A 2-point special earn rate is compared with your tier base rate rather than multiplying that base rate by 2; caps and eligibility also matter.

Can gift cards count?

They may not count at purchase time in the way users expect. Check the local rule and compare with the gift-card guide.

Is 2x enough for Platinum or Diamond?

It depends on missing points. Use the calculator result with your current Play Points progress.
