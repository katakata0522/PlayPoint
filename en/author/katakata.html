About Katakata & editorial standards

Last updated 2026-08-28

Katakata operates the Google Play Points Calculator and edits guides about Play Points calculations, usage, regional differences, and troubleshooting.

How guides are verified

  • Google Play Help and other first-party sources are checked first.
  • Rules that can vary by country, account, or time are described with those limits instead of being presented as universal.
  • Calculation examples are checked against the calculator settings used on this site.
  • When an important rule changes, the affected guide and its verification date are reviewed.

Checks before publication

  1. Match the claim, source, and verification date against first-party information.
  2. Reproduce calculation examples in the calculator and confirm the result.
  3. Run automated checks for calculations, internal links, structured data, and generated pages.
  4. After deployment, verify that key production pages and assets are serving the intended revision.

Corrections and updates

Google Play Points benefits and rules can change. If a guide is incorrect or outdated, the page is reviewed and corrected rather than silently relying on old assumptions.

Regional rule

The language of a page does not determine Play Points rules. Your Google Play country, account, current level, eligible purchase, and the offer shown in Google Play are the final conditions to check.
